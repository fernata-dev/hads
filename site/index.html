02 / files

It has seen every folder. So a copy takes three keys.

A gentle background index of your home folder feeds the predictor. Type cp rep and hads writes the file and the place you always copy it to.

  • 01Indexes up to 250,000 files at low priority, and rescans every 30 minutes.
  • 02Jumps anywhere you've been: cd web becomes cd ~/code/webapp.
  • 03hads find taxes searches your disk in about a millisecond.

03 / smart mode

Say it in Arabic or English. Review the plan, then it runs.

Off until you add your OpenRouter key. Type ? and a request. hads reads your OS and package managers, plans exact commands, flags risky ones, blocks destructive ones, and repairs a step that fails.

  • ✓Plans for this machine: pacman on Arch, apt on Ubuntu, brew on macOS, winget on Windows.
  • ✓Nothing runs until you approve: all at once, step by step, or cancel.
  • ✓A local guard the model can't override blocks rm -rf /, mkfs and their kin.

04 / privacy

Your history stays yours. Nothing to sign up for.

Prediction runs entirely on your machine. The only network call is smart mode, and only when you turn it on.

◎On-device model

History, index and model live in your config folder with 0600 permissions, behind a user-only socket.

✕Secrets never stored

Tokens, passwords, API keys and auth headers are dropped before they reach the model or the disk.

#Verified installs

The installer checks every binary against its SHA-256 and refuses on a mismatch. MIT-licensed, open source.

06 / faq

Questions, answered.

Is it a large language model?

No. Prediction is a small statistical model trained only on your commands, which is why it answers in microseconds and needs no internet. Smart mode alone uses an LLM, through OpenRouter.

Will it slow my shell down?

Predictions are fetched asynchronously in zsh, so typing never waits. A full round trip takes about 4 ms, even on a 2014 laptop.

I already use zsh-autosuggestions.

Keep it. hads registers itself as its first strategy instead of replacing it.

How do I remove it?

hads uninstall --purge removes the hooks, the autostart and everything it learned.
