Smart mode

Just say it. In Arabic or English.

Type a request instead of a command, no prefix needed. hads plans the exact commands for your OS, shows them, and runs only after you say yes. Free models work out of the box.

  • ✓Plans for this machine: pacman on Arch, apt on Ubuntu, brew on macOS, winget on Windows.
  • ✓Nothing runs until you approve it: all at once, step by step, or cancel.
  • ✓A local guard the model can't override blocks destructive commands.

Questions, answered.

Is it a large language model?

No. Prediction is a small statistical model trained only on your commands, so it answers in microseconds with no internet. Only smart mode uses an LLM, through OpenRouter.

Do I have to pay for smart mode?

No. Free OpenRouter models work, and hads falls back to a free model automatically if yours fails.

Will it slow my shell?

No. In zsh predictions arrive in the background; a full round trip is about 4 ms even on old hardware.

How do I remove it?

hads uninstall --purge removes the hooks, the autostart and everything it learned.
